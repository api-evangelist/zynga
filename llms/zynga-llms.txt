# Zynga

> Zynga is a mobile and social game developer and publisher, founded in 2007 in San
> Francisco and a wholly owned subsidiary of Take-Two Interactive since May 2022.
> It operates free-to-play franchises including FarmVille, Zynga Poker, Words With
> Friends, CSR Racing, Empires & Puzzles, Toon Blast, Merge Dragons, Harry Potter:
> Puzzles & Spells, Top Eleven and Golf Rival.

## API surface

Zynga operates **no public developer program**. As of 2026-09-13 there is no
developer portal, no API reference, no published OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/gRPC/WSDL
contract, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no llms.txt of its own, and no
first-party SDK in any package registry.

- `api.zynga.com` resolves and answers over HTTPS, but it is an internal service
  router for Zynga's own game clients: every path returns HTTP 400 with
  `error-category: pbr.request.malformed` / `error-message: Failed to extract
  service name`, and its `robots.txt` is `Disallow: /` for all user agents except
  `facebookexternalhit`. It is not a documented public API.
- `developers.zynga.com`, `developer.zynga.com` and `docs.zynga.com` have no address
  records (NODATA) — they do not exist as hosts.
- The third-party "Zynga API" announced at Zynga Unleashed in 2011-2012 was retired
  along with the zynga.com third-party publishing platform.
- Chartboost, the mobile advertising and monetization platform with a public App
  Management API, was acquired by Zynga in 2021 and **sold to LoopMe in December
  2024**. Its APIs are no longer a Zynga surface and are not attributed here.
